## Flagship: Homeowner Options Navigator

A private, spreadsheet-style comparison for homeowners facing time-sensitive mortgage difficulty. It organizes reinstatement, lender-provided forbearance or modification terms, sale with estimated equity, short-sale implications, counseling pathways, carrying costs, deadlines, risks, and missing information.

<Warning>The app must not predict lender approval, provide legal or tax advice, or route passive exploration to investors.</Warning>

## Plumbing donor: Instant Value and Equity

The first vertical slice establishes address lookup, property data, value ranges, equity math, consent, lead routing, and report delivery needed by the flagship.

## Core utility: Housing Decision Lab

One calculation engine supports Payment/Affordability, Buy Before You Sell, Balanced Renter-to-Owner, Right-Sizing, and Stay-and-Modify versus Move.

## Careful experiment: Private Sale Path

Compare verified private, pre-market, cash, investor, renovation-partner, and full-market paths. Disclose trade-offs in exposure, competition, price discovery, certainty, timing, fees, and repairs. Build consented buyer demand before launching seller matching.

## Supporting experiences

- Listing Relaunch Lab separates observed facts from hypotheses.
- Explainable visual preference discovery can establish buyer demand later.
- AI chat may support a defined workflow but is not a standalone product.

## Rejected patterns

- Silent Seller Radar or hidden readiness scoring
- Distressed-property opportunity maps
- Fake urgency or scarcity
- Exact valuations or false-precision forecasts
- Renting-equals-zero comparisons
- Unverified secret-buyer claims
