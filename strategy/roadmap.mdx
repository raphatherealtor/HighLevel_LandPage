## Gate 0: Verify

- Confirm property-data display, caching, attribution, and derivative-use rights.
- Send one non-production Figgy-to-BoldTrail contact and document field mapping.
- Confirm inbound CRM routing, duplicate behavior, and errors.
- Approve SMS consent before enabling messaging.
- Freeze the first-page promise, audience, and conversion action.

## Phase 1: Instant Value and Equity

Build the narrow plumbing donor and minimum reusable financial primitives.

## Phase 2: Homeowner Options Navigator

Reuse property, equity, consent, and delivery foundations. Add lender-provided inputs, carrying-cost math, scenario comparison, counseling resources, and escalation boundaries.

## Phase 3: Housing Decision Lab

Release Payment/Affordability, Buy Before You Sell, Balanced Renter-to-Owner, and Right-Sizing without duplicating calculations.

## Phase 4: Demand and matching

Pilot buyer preference capture, verification, consent, and expiry. Launch seller matching only after genuine current demand exists.

## Phase 5: Segmented tools

Add Listing Relaunch and other focused campaigns based on qualified outcomes.

<Note>DecisionOS and HomeTwin are the long-term consolidation path. They do not block the first micro-app.</Note>
