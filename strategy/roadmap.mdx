## Gate 0: Verify

- Preserve the written REcore/CRMLS confirmation that public display is permitted when licensed-name, DRE-number, brokerage, attribution, and applicable advertising disclosures are present. Confirm the exact required presentation plus caching, retention, and derivative-use rules before connecting live data.
- Approve a field-level allowlist proving that every provider-derived property fact is publicly obtainable; reject private fields server-side. Preserve provenance as public source, user-provided, or calculated.
- Complete the Figgy structured non-production ingestion test and document field mapping, consent preservation, idempotency, duplicate behavior, errors, and retries. A first test contact reached BoldTrail successfully on 2026-07-22. Test the direct API only if it remains a serious candidate or record why Figgy was selected without adding a redundant route.
- Approve SMS consent before enabling messaging.
- Freeze the first-page promise, audience, and conversion action.
- Approve the daily provider-spend budget, alert recipients, lookup limits, bot mitigation, and contract-permitted cache TTL.
- Assign the privacy-policy reviewer and approve the policy before external preview.

## Phase 1: Instant Value and Equity

Build the narrow plumbing donor and minimum reusable financial primitives.

## Phase 2: Homeowner Options Navigator

Reuse property, equity, consent, and delivery foundations. Add lender-provided inputs, carrying-cost math, scenario comparison, counseling resources, and escalation boundaries.

## Phase 3: Housing Decision Lab

Release Payment/Affordability, Buy Before You Sell, Balanced Renter-to-Owner, and Right-Sizing without duplicating calculations.

## Phase 4: Demand and matching

Pilot buyer preference capture, verification, consent, and expiry. Launch seller matching only after genuine current demand exists.

## Phase 5: Segmented tools

Add Listing Relaunch and other focused campaigns based on qualified outcomes.
