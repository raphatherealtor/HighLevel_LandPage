## Desired impression

Calm, premium, precise, and human. The experience should feel like a trusted financial decision tool, not an aggressive funnel or generic real-estate template.

## Approved visual direction

The selected family is a cinematic **living property portrait**:

- Midnight-navy architectural environment with restrained carbon texture
- Coastal property photography dissolving into parcel geometry and public-data linework
- Electro-blue and electro-white information light
- Editorial serif headlines paired with a precise geometric sans-serif interface
- Brushed brass limited primarily to the RK mark, hairline separators, and quiet material highlights
- One dominant visual and one dominant action per screen

This direction must remain a homeowner intelligence experience. It must not drift into a luxury-listing catalog, agent-first dashboard, or decorative concept that hides inputs, sources, assumptions, or uncertainty.

## Approved ownership sequence

Both reference screens are approved because they serve consecutive states:

1. **Public/private diptych:** establishes the ownership threshold and keeps the public result available.
2. **Guided refinement:** previews the optional private chapters after the visitor has crossed that threshold.

The implementation must not combine them into one overloaded screen. No private field is enabled before the visitor affirms, “I confirm that I personally own this property.” The adjacent explanation must say that this is the visitor's representation, not independent title verification.

## Approved refinement sequence

The refinement experience uses two approved screens with separate jobs:

1. **Four-Chapter Refinement:** the guided data-entry flow. Present one calm chapter at a time—property condition, improvements, mortgage/payoff, and selling-cost assumptions—with clear progress, optional fields, and an immediate explanation of what each answer changes.
2. **Private Decision Sheet:** the consolidated review and output state. Show homeowner inputs, their provenance, what each input affects, the refined value range, estimated equity, and estimated net proceeds together before the visitor requests delivery or advisor help.

Do not merge these into one crowded screen. Every private input must remain optional, correctable, visibly labeled as user-provided, and covered by the confirmed-ownership boundary.

The calculation boundary must remain explicit:

- Condition and supported improvements may refine the estimated value range.
- Mortgage payoff affects estimated equity and net proceeds, not the property's value.
- Selling-cost assumptions affect estimated net proceeds, not the property's value.
- Unknown inputs must remain unknown or use a clearly labeled editable assumption; the interface must never imply invented precision.

## Approved final delivery screen

The selected final state is the **Private Portfolio** direction. It completes the value exchange before offering human help:

1. Show the homeowner's refined value, equity, and proceeds as ranges with the same provenance and uncertainty rules used throughout the experience.
2. Make the private report preview and report delivery the dominant visual and primary action.
3. Keep **Download a summary now** and **Review my inputs** available without forcing a consultation request.
4. Present advisor contact as a separate, optional, unchecked choice: **I'd like Raphael to contact me about my options.**
5. State beside that choice that the report can be delivered without requesting a consultation.

Entering an email and requesting the report authorizes only delivery and operational messages necessary to fulfill that request. It must not silently authorize marketing, telephone calls, text messages, or enrollment in a nurture campaign. Any broader contact permission requires its own clear, unchecked consent control and auditable consent record.

The production screen must use final reviewed legal language and accurate brokerage disclosures. The reference image establishes hierarchy, atmosphere, and interaction boundaries; sample figures and placeholder legal copy are not production content.

## Logo treatment

Preserve the original RK geometry, typography, wording, metallic construction, and eXp/Southern California lockup. Integrate the plaque by shifting only the black field, carbon surround, and atmospheric shadow into midnight navy, with a restrained electro-blue edge reflection.

The generated treatment below is the approved **material and color reference**, not permission to retype or AI-redraw the production logo. Production must use the highest-fidelity original or an authorized source asset and must be checked letter-for-letter.

## Principles

- Hierarchy before decoration
- Warm restraint rather than sterile fintech
- Generous whitespace around important decisions
- Dense details only inside structured comparison surfaces
- Visible evidence and assumptions
- Motion that explains real state changes
- No artificial urgency

## Tokens still requiring exact values

Palette, typography and numeric style, spacing, radius, borders/shadows, button hierarchy, fields, card/table density, chart colors, icons, and motion.

## Required reference screens

Approve desktop and mobile versions of address entry, value/equity result, future spreadsheet-style Options Navigator, loading, partial-data, low-confidence, and provider-failure states.

## Accessibility baseline

WCAG AA contrast, keyboard access, visible focus, semantic labels, linked errors, reduced motion, no color-only meaning, and readable financial tables at 200% zoom.

The exact Lovable prompt is generated only after references and tokens are approved. Every micro-app must inherit the same rules.
