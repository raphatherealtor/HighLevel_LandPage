**Status:** Proposed. Requires Rapha and Claude review before application generation.

## Audience and promise

A homeowner curious about current value, equity, or possible proceeds who wants orientation without waiting for a callback.

> See a preliminary home-value and equity range, understand what affects it, and choose whether to request a deeper review.

## Value exchange

1. Enter an address.
2. Retrieve permitted property information.
3. Show useful preliminary information.
4. Let optional corrections refine the range.
5. Offer detailed comps, net proceeds, or advisor follow-up.
6. State which answers accompany the request.

## Included

- Address normalization and permitted property lookup
- Property summary confirmation
- Preliminary value range, source date, and confidence treatment
- Optional condition corrections
- User-entered mortgage balance and estimated equity
- Simple net-proceeds range
- Transparent lead and consent capture
- Verified Figgy/BoldTrail/API delivery
- Mobile, loading, partial-data, low-confidence, and failure states

## Excluded

- Exact valuation, user accounts, HomeTwin persistence, and AI chat
- Appreciation forecasts or automatic refinance advice
- Full sell/rent/keep comparison or pre-foreclosure logic
- Automatic SMS without approved consent
- Public display of unapproved MLS data
- Private MLS fields, non-public provider enrichment, inferred debt or distress, and non-public facts about property the visitor does not personally own

## Success measures

Measure address-to-result completion, result-to-request conversion, valid delivery, CRM completeness, duplicate rate, qualified conversations, appointments, consumer corrections, provider failures, cache rate, blocked abuse, and provider spend. Form conversion alone is insufficient.

A **qualified conversation** is a reply or live exchange in which the person's identity and property interest are confirmed and they ask a substantive valuation, equity, proceeds, or decision question, or accept a detailed review. Automated opens, invalid contacts, wrong numbers, and unsubscribe-only replies do not qualify.

## Performance budgets

- Mobile LCP: **2.5 seconds or less at the 75th percentile** on the agreed mobile test profile.
- Initial compressed transfer: **500 KB or less**, excluding lazy-loaded maps, reports, and third-party challenge assets.
- Cached property result: **1.5 seconds or less at p75** after address normalization.
- Uncached provider result: **4 seconds or less at p75**; show honest progress after 800 ms and a recoverable timeout by 10 seconds.

Record performance separately for cached, uncached, partial, and failed responses. Provider behavior may force revision before launch, but no budget may be removed without a dated decision.
