<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter address">Normalize and confirm the property without requesting contact information.</Step>
  <Step title="Confirm property facts">Show permitted facts and let the visitor correct supported fields.</Step>
  <Step title="Show preliminary result">Present a value range, source date, confidence, and influential factors.</Step>
  <Step title="Confirm ownership for private inputs">Before accepting non-public property facts, ask the visitor to affirm that they personally own the property. Keep the public preliminary result available without this attestation.</Step>
  <Step title="Refine voluntarily">For an owner who attests, offer optional condition or renovation corrections, label them user-provided, and show what changed.</Step>
  <Step title="Estimate equity and proceeds">Ask for mortgage balance only when equity analysis opens. Label it user-provided and optional. Provide **I don't know my payoff** and allow either skipping equity or entering an explicitly approximate balance/range.</Step>
  <Step title="Request deeper analysis">State the deliverable, fields, delivery channel, transmitted information, and follow-up expectation.</Step>
  <Step title="Confirm delivery">Show what was requested, how it arrives, and how to correct or stop follow-up.</Step>
</Steps>

## Required states

- **Loading:** honest progress, timeout, and recovery.
- **Partial data:** identify missing facts and allow corrections.
- **Low confidence:** explain the broad range and offer review.
- **Unsupported property:** never fabricate a result.
- **No ownership attestation:** show only the permitted public-data experience; do not accept or retain non-public property facts.
- **Provider failure:** retry safely; do not create a lead without an intentional request.
- **Duplicate:** apply the [15-minute idempotency contract](/phase-1/data-and-integrations#idempotency-contract), return the original receipt, and avoid repeated contacts, campaign enrollment, reports, or messages.
- **Rate or budget limited:** show the documented high-demand state and manual-review alternative without repeating the paid lookup.

## Mobile requirements

Use one primary action per viewport, large controls, visible assumptions, collapsible scenario details, keyboard-safe inputs, and visible focus. Avoid precision dragging or wide financial tables.
