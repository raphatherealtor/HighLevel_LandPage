## Rules

- Keep calculations deterministic and testable.
- Record source dates and user assumptions.
- Never let an LLM produce authoritative financial numbers.
- Present ranges when inputs or evidence are uncertain.
- Keep display rounding separate from stored precision.

## Preliminary value contract

The provider and permitted method remain **open**. The response must include lower estimate, optional central estimate, upper estimate, confidence/evidence tier, effective date, displayable sources, and assumptions.

## Estimated equity

```text
estimated equity = estimated property value - user-entered secured debt balance
```

Apply the debt balance to both range bounds.

<Warning>Equity is not available cash. It excludes sale costs, unentered liens, taxes, payoff changes, and lender requirements.</Warning>

## Estimated net proceeds

```text
estimated net proceeds = estimated sale price
                       - mortgage and entered liens
                       - estimated selling costs
                       - entered repairs or concessions
                       - other disclosed deductions
```

Do not hard-code a universal commission or closing-cost percentage. Use editable assumptions and never present negotiable fees as fixed.

## Golden cases required

- No mortgage
- Mortgage below the low estimate
- Mortgage inside the value range
- Negative estimated equity
- Missing payoff
- Editable selling-cost assumptions
- Invalid and extreme inputs
