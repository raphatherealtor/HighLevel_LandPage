title: "Consent and trust contract"
description: "The boundary between useful lead context and covert profiling"
---

This is a legitimate lead-generation experience. It may exchange a deeper report or advisor help for contact information when the exchange is stated plainly.

## Permitted submitted context

- Contact details and property address
- Requested deliverable
- Submitted corrections, selected scenario, and needed assumptions
- Contact preference and consent receipt

## Prohibited context

- Undisclosed cross-session history
- HomeTwin activity
- Passive distress or readiness classification
- Sensitive third-party enrichment unrelated to the request
- Investor routing the homeowner did not request

## Before submission, state

- What the visitor receives
- Which information is sent and to whom
- How it may be used for follow-up
- Which delivery channel is used
- Whether SMS consent is optional or required
- How to correct information or stop follow-up

Mortgage balance and hardship details require conspicuous explanation, minimization, and a retention purpose.

## Privacy-policy ownership

- Accountable owner: **Rapha**.
- Required reviewer: a designated privacy/legal reviewer appropriate to the launch jurisdiction.
- Required artifact: public privacy policy covering collected fields, purposes, processors, retention, contact method, correction/deletion requests, analytics, and text/email practices.
- Gate: policy URL and review record are required **before the first external preview**, not merely before production.
