## Blocking application generation

1. Which REcore/CRMLS feed, endpoint, or approved provider powers the preliminary result?
2. What exact attribution language, fields, caching TTL, retention, and derivative-calculation rules apply beyond the confirmed public-display permission?
3. Does Figgy preserve every required field, consent receipt, stable request ID, retry, and duplicate rule in the structured verification?
4. Which contact fields are required?
5. What exactly is the detailed deliverable?
6. Is SMS included, and what consent applies?
7. Which brand identity and visual references govern the family?
8. Who is the designated privacy/legal reviewer, and what policy URL will be used before external preview?
9. What daily lookup budget, alert recipients, and provider-permitted cache TTL are approved?
10. What exact licensed name, DRE number, brokerage identity, logos, and equal-housing or other required disclosures must appear in the shared site footer and result views?
