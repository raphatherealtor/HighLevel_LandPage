## Product

- Preliminary results provide value before capture.
- Deliverable and follow-up expectations are explicit.
- Unsupported properties never receive fabricated values.
- Low confidence is visible.

## Calculation

- Every formula has unit tests and manually reviewed golden examples.
- Both range bounds, negative equity, missing payoff, and editable assumptions behave correctly.
- No LLM generates authoritative numbers.

## Integration

- Provider responses match the documented contract.
- Secrets are absent from browser bundles and history.
- One request creates one CRM contact/update.
- Repeated submissions are idempotent.
- Failures are observable and recoverable.
- Consent and source attribution survive ingestion.

## Experience and safety

- Desktop/mobile match approved references.
- Keyboard and screen-reader smoke tests pass.
- Loading corresponds to real work.
- Sensitive fields and logs are minimized.
- Preview and production are separate.
- HomeTwin data is never queried.
