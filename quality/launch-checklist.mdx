---
title: "Launch checklist"
description: "Controlled preview, production, rollback, and first-week review"
---

## Before preview

- Specification and visual references approved
- Provider and CRM spikes passed
- Consent copy approved
- Privacy policy reviewed and published
- Environment ownership documented

## Before production

- Production values, domain, redirects, and analytics confirmed
- Error monitoring enabled
- Rate limits, bot challenge, cache policy, spend alerts, and daily cap validated
- Performance budgets passed in preview
- CRM campaign limited or manually supervised
- Test data removed
- Privacy/contact links present
- Rollback tested
- Final mobile review complete

## Controlled release

1. Internal review.
2. Scripted test submissions.
3. Small organic traffic.
4. Error and qualified-conversation review.
5. Paid traffic only after delivery and follow-up are reliable.

Review provider failures, duplicates, invalid leads, corrections, qualified conversations, delivery time, mobile abandonment, confusing assumptions, and unexpected automation. Do not optimize raw form conversion before quality and trust are understood.
